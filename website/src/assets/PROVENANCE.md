# Published assets

- `manrope.woff2`: unmodified Latin variable Manrope font from Google Fonts, downloaded October 9, 2026. SIL Open Font License in `Manrope-LICENSE.txt`. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope.
- `montserrat.woff2` and `onest.woff2`: unmodified Latin variable fonts from Google Fonts, downloaded October 10, 2026. SIL Open Font Licenses in `Montserrat-LICENSE.txt` and `Onest-LICENSE.txt`. Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/montserrat and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/onest.
- `chai-background.svg`: unchanged backdrop asset from the official ChaiUI registry, https://ui.chaicode.com/r/backdrop.json, downloaded October 10, 2026. ChaiUI styling and the half-circle theme icon are adapted from its published theme and theme-switcher registry items for this static website.
- `synapse-overview.png`, `customization_options.png`, and `usuage_stats.png`: unchanged copies of the Synapse repository's root `assets/` product captures. Usage figures are example local estimates, not product prices or a provider invoice.
- `synapse-icon.png`: unchanged copy of `synapse/src/assets/synapse.png`.
- The interactive wheel uses the app's eight SVG icon paths and radial geometry from `synapse/src/wedges.ts`. It is an explanatory website guide, not an embedded desktop runtime.

No generated concept art or remote font/image service is included in the production website.
