From a thought
to the place it belongs.

Keep working in the app you already have open. Synapse brings the next action to you, then gets out of the way.

  1. Bring up the wheel

    Press Ctrl+Alt+Enter. The menu appears at your cursor, ready for you to choose a tool.

  2. Choose Speech-to-Text and speak

    Dictation listens until you press Enter or click the listening circle to finish. Optional local dictation uses an approximately 630 MB model download.

  3. Put your words straight to work

    Synapse restores your previous app’s focus and pastes the transcription where you were typing.

A place for the useful bits

Notes Hub keeps rich notes, folders, and sticky notes locally. Clipboard history lets you search, pin, and paste saved text.

Capture what’s in view

Screenshot captures the monitor under your cursor, saves a PNG in Pictures/Synapse, and copies it to your clipboard.

Listen, ask, or wrap up

Speak Selected Text reads your selection aloud. AI opens a voice orb and streams a provider’s reply. Force Quit exits Synapse itself.

A few things
before you begin.

What runs locally?

Clipboard history and notes are stored locally. Dictation uses an optional local speech model, around 630 MB to download. AI chat sends your conversation to the provider you choose.

Do I need an AI subscription?

AI chat uses your own API key for Anthropic, OpenAI, or OpenRouter. API usage is billed separately by your provider. The Usage page shows local estimates, not a provider invoice.

What will Synapse cost?

The current Windows release is available on GitHub. Paid licensing is planned as one purchase per major version, with optional paid upgrades to future major versions. Price, included features, and timing are still to be announced.
