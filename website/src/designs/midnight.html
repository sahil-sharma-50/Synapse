From a passing thought
to words on the page.

01

Call up the wheel.

Press Ctrl + Alt + Enter. Synapse opens at your cursor. Choose Speech-to-Text to start dictating.

02

Say what you mean.

Speak naturally. Dictation keeps listening until you press Enter or click the listening circle to finish.

03

Pick up where you left off.

Synapse transcribes your recording and pastes the words into the app you were using before opening the wheel.

Keep the useful bits.

Search, pin and paste from local clipboard history. Keep rich notes in folders, or open individual notes as sticky windows on your desktop.

Capture what’s in front of you.

Screenshot captures the monitor under your cursor, saves a PNG to Pictures/Synapse, and copies it to your clipboard.

Your AI.
A clearer view of usage.

Connect your own Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter key. Responses stream into the AI panel, with optional spoken replies. Local usage history helps you follow requests, tokens and estimated costs.

A few things
before you begin.

What runs locally?

Clipboard history and notes are stored locally. Dictation uses an optional local speech model, around 630 MB to download. AI chat sends your conversation to the provider you choose.

Do I need an AI subscription?

AI chat uses your own API key for Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter. API usage is billed separately by your provider. The Usage page shows local estimates, not a provider invoice.

What will Synapse cost?

The current Windows release is available on GitHub. Paid licensing is planned as one purchase per major version, with optional paid upgrades to future major versions. Price, included features and timing are still to be announced.
